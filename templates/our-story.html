Our Story

Three generations.
One philosophy.
A lifetime devoted to the art of Japanese hospitality.

THIRD GENERATION

A Legacy That Began
Long Before Us

Matsumoto was never created to become the busiest restaurant in the city. It began as a small family kitchen nestled among the quiet landscapes of Shibuya, where every meal was prepared with patience, intention, and respect for the changing seasons.

What started with a single craftsman slowly became a family tradition, passed from one generation to the next—not through written recipes, but through years spent standing beside parents, grandparents, and mentors, learning that every dish carries the responsibility of the family name.

Today, the third generation continues that same philosophy, preserving timeless techniques while embracing thoughtful refinement for a new generation of guests.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Simplicity Is
The Highest Form
Of Luxury

True luxury is never loud. It exists in quiet confidence, careful craftsmanship, and thoughtful hospitality.

Every ingredient is selected with purpose. Every plate is composed with restraint. Every guest is welcomed as though they have returned home after a long journey.

At Matsumoto, we believe that remarkable dining is not created through excess, but through balance, precision, and unwavering attention to the smallest details.

SEASONAL INGREDIENTS

Guided By
The Rhythm
Of Nature

The finest ingredient is never the rarest— it is the one harvested at precisely the right moment.

Our menu evolves with the seasons, celebrating the quiet beauty of spring, the freshness of summer, the richness of autumn, and the comforting warmth of winter.

Rather than forcing nature to adapt to us, we allow each season to shape the experience we create for every guest.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

Every Detail
Matters

Behind every plate lies years of discipline, countless hours of refinement, and an unwavering pursuit of excellence.

From the sharpening of every knife to the placement of every garnish, each decision reflects the belief that perfection is found in the smallest details.

Our guests may remember the flavors, but it is the care behind each moment that leaves a lasting impression.

THE EXPERIENCE

More Than
A Meal

Dining at Matsumoto is designed to slow the pace of everyday life.

Soft lighting, carefully selected music, attentive yet unobtrusive service, and thoughtfully paced courses come together to create an evening that encourages conversation, connection, and appreciation.

Every reservation is an invitation to pause, celebrate, and share meaningful moments around the table.
